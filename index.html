🏛️ Place Value Studio

Interactive tools to explore numbers, addition, subtraction, multiplication & division
🔢
Single Number
Every digit’s place gives it value – a hundred is always 100, no matter where it sits!
➕
Addition Lab
Addition is putting groups together – and regrouping 10 ones makes a whole new ten!
➖
Subtraction Lab
When you don’t have enough ones, you can “borrow” a ten – like exchanging a coin for smaller ones.
✖️
Multiplication Lab
Multiplication is repeated addition – 4 × 3 means 4 groups of 3, which is the same as 3+3+3+3!
➗
Division Lab
Division splits a number into equal groups – sometimes a few leftovers (remainder) stay behind.
📝
Custom Test
Create your own test paper! Choose any operation (+, -, ×, ÷) and set the difficulty – then challenge yourself.
📝
Patterns
Create your own patterns worksheet! Choose any level and combinations – then challenge yourself.
